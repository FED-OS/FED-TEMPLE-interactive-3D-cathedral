# Pricing

## ✦ FED-TEMPLE is free. Period.

There is no pricing page because there is no price. FED-TEMPLE is **100% free, forever**, under the [MIT License](LICENSE).

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|------|-------|--------------|
| **Everyone** | **$0** | The entire app. Every feature. No limits we control. No account. No tracking. |

### Why is there no paid tier?

Because FED-TEMPLE is a static HTML file. There is no server to meter, no database to provision, no compute to charge for. Charging for it would be charging for nothing — which is, frankly, bullshit, and we don't do bullshit.

The only "cost" is GitHub's own rate limit (60 unauthenticated API calls/hour per IP), which is *their* policy, not ours. We engineer around it with caching and scoped fetching. Optional PAT support on the roadmap will let power users lift that limit themselves, at no cost from us.
