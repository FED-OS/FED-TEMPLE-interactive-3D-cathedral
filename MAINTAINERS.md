## ✦ Maintainer responsibilities

- Triage and label incoming issues and PRs within a reasonable window.
- Review PRs against the [ethos](CONTRIBUTING.md#the-ethos) and the code conventions in [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) / [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md).
- Merge approved PRs; keep `main` green and deployable.
- Cut releases, update [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md), tag versions.
- Guard scope: reject dependency sprawl, required backends, telemetry, and build-step creep.
- Record non-trivial decisions in [ADR.md](ADR.md).
- Be responsive in Discussions; keep the community informed via [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) updates.

## ✦ Emeritus maintainers

Maintainers who step down or are inactive for 6+ months move to emeritus status. They keep all credit (and remain in [AUTHORS.md](AUTHORS.md)) but relinquish active commit access. They can return by re-confirming with the active team.

## ✦ Becoming a maintainer

- Nominated by an existing maintainer.
- Lazy-consensus period of one week (see [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md#decision-making)).
- Demonstrated through sustained, ethos-aligned contributions and constructive review participation.
